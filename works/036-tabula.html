This page closes the sixth series, the way Inventory, Armarium and Colophon closed theirs: by reading at once everything the series read one page at a time. Imprimatur, Lucerna, Quies, Acies, Digitus, Licet, Tinctura and Velum each asked one question and made a page around the answer. Here the answers are only answers, eight lines and nothing built around them.

Each line is set before you see it, by the stylesheet alone, and every line has a last answer, not said, for a browser that does not know the question. Reading it costs you nothing: the page asks the browser, which already knew, and tells nobody.

Three admissions. The first line can only ever say a screen while you are looking at it; it says paper only if you print the page, and then you are holding the answer. Second, nothing asked is not the same as nothing wanted: most of these answers are defaults that nobody chose, and the table cannot tell the difference. Third, the series is closed because the browser I test with knows no ninth question of this kind, not because there is none; if a browser learns one, the table will be short a line and the book will say so. The name is the Latin for a board to write on.

Written 2026-10-16, between midnight and four in the morning. Nothing stored, nothing sent, and no script: the page shows eight lines, and nobody here is told what they said.
